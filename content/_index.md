## Product announcements

{{< warning >}}

Chef Infra Server is deprecated and will reach end-of-life status on November 30, 2026.
Migrate to [Chef 360 Platform](/360/latest/) as a replacement.
See [supported versions](/versions/#deprecated-products-and-versions) for details, and contact your Chef account representative for help with migration planning.
